# CAR DOMINOES (TRAFFIC SLAM!)
## Official Rulebook & Tabletop Guide (Ages 5+)

> **The chaotic mix-and-match domino card game where traffic jams turn into Franken-Cars, and every true match triggers a high-speed Turbo Boost!**

---

## 1. Game Summary

* **Target Audience:** Ages 5+ (Kids & Family Game Night)
* **Players:** 2 to 4 Players
* **Game Duration:** 10 to 15 Minutes
* **Core Mechanisms:** Domino-Style Matching, Hand Shedding, Adjacent Edge-Connecting, 3D Overlapping/Stacking, Pattern Recognition.
* **Objective:** Be the first driver to shed all cards from your hand and cross the **Checkered Flag**!

---

## 2. Components & Deck Anatomy

The game is played with a **36-card deck** (precisely four 3×3 printable poker-size sheets).

### The 5 Vehicles & Color Codes
Every vehicle has an instantly recognizable solid background color and a signature kid sound effect:

| Vehicle | Background Color | Visual Features | Sound Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fire Truck** | **Bold Red** | Big extension ladder, water hose, siren light | *"WEE-WOO!"* |
| **Police Cruiser** | **Vivid Blue** | Flashing lightbar, star badge, grille guard | *"WOOP-WOOP!"* |
| **School Bus** | **Bright Yellow** | Bus windows, black warning stripes, stop sign | *"HONK-HONK!"* |
| **Monster Truck** | **Lime Green** | Gigantic muddy off-road tires, flame decals | *"ROAAAR!"* |
| **Race Car** | **Hot Orange** | Aerodynamic spoiler, racing stripes, #1 decals | *"VROOOOM!"* |

---

### Card Formats & Cut Seams

Each card is split into two halves (Left Half & Right Half):

```
TYPE A: EDGE-SEAM (Inward Cut)           TYPE B: CENTER-SEAM (Franken-Card)
┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┐ ┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
│     RED REAR     │    BLUE FRONT    │ │     RED REAR     │    BLUE FRONT    │
│(Cut at Left Edge)│(Cut at Right Edge│ │(Cut at Center)   │(Cut at Center)   │
│ ◄──[SEAM]        │       [SEAM]───► │ │         ▲ [SEAM] │                  │
└──────────────────┴──────────────────┘ └─────────┴────────┴──────────────────┘
  Best for ADJACENT edge-connections!       Best for OVERLAPPING on top!
```

1. **Center-Seam Halves:** The two halves meet at the card's center line. When played, one half is dropped **on top** of a matching colored half on the table (Overlapping Play).
2. **Edge-Seam Halves:** The car half is cut off at the card's outer perimeter. To complete this car, another card must be placed **adjacent** (edge-to-edge) against that rim.
3. **90° Corner Cuts:** Some halves feature cars driving across the short axis or turning. Connecting to them requires placing your card at a **90-degree angle**, creating T-bones and corners that keep the traffic jam wrapping neatly within the table!

## 3. Setup

1. **Shuffle the 36-card deck.**
2. **Deal 5 cards** face-down to each player (players hold their cards in hand).
3. Place the remaining cards face-down in the center of the table as the **Junkyard** (draw pile).
4. Flip the top card of the Junkyard face-up into the middle of the table. This is the **Starting Street**.
5. The youngest player goes first. Play proceeds clockwise.

---

## 4. How to Play

On your turn, you must play **1 card** from your hand if you have a legal match.

### The Golden Rule: Color-to-Color
You can attach your card to **any exposed half of the matching background color** on the table. You may play it in one of two ways:

```
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAY MOVE 1: ADJACENT (Expand the Street)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Place your card side-by-side against an open matching color with an Edge-Seam.
• Red meets Red at the edge.
• The road spreads out across the table, creating intersections and turns.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAY MOVE 2: OVERLAP (Pile Up the Jam)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Drop your matching color half directly ON TOP of an exposed matching half.
• Red covers Red.
• The covered half is buried. Your other half extends out into a new space!
• Keeps the traffic jam compact and builds funny multi-level Franken-cars!
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

---

### Drawing from the Junkyard
If you have **no legal plays** in your hand (or choose not to play):
1. Draw **1 card** from the Junkyard into your hand.
2. If the drawn card can be played legally right now, you may play it immediately!
3. If not, say *"HONK!"* and your turn ends.

---

## 5. The Big Twist: "TRUE MATCH TURBO BOOST!"

Most plays create funny **Franken-Cars** (e.g., Red Fire Truck Rear + Blue Police Front), which stay on the board and add to the chaotic jam.

However, when a player aligns a **True Match** (the matching front and rear of the **exact same vehicle**):

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    *** TRUE MATCH ALARM! ***                    │
│                                                                 │
│   [ BLUE POLICE REAR ]  +  [ BLUE POLICE FRONT ]                │
│                                                                 │
│   1. YELL: The player makes the vehicle sound:                  │
│      "WOOP-WOOP! OUTTA MY WAY!"                                 │
│   2. DRIVE OFF: The completed car revs its engine and is placed │
│      into the Scrap Pile (instantly clearing table space)!      │
│   3. TURBO BOOST: The player immediately plays a SECOND card   │
│      from their hand!                                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> [!TIP]
> **Kid Advantage & Fair Start:** Kids love hunting for True Matches. Pulling off a Turbo Boost lets them shed 2 cards in a single turn!
> * **Warm-Up Lap Rule:** On the very first turn of the game, a True Match still clears the car into the Scrap Pile, but the Turbo Boost bonus play is saved for Turn 2 onward so both players get a fair start out of the gates! (Validated by Monte Carlo simulation: keeps win rate at a balanced 52% vs 48%).

## 6. Special Action Cards

The deck includes 4 high-energy action cards designed for 5-year-olds:

* **The Tow Truck (2 Cards - Rainbow Wildcard):**
  * The Tow Truck has a giant yellow crane hook on a rainbow background.
  * It can be placed onto **ANY color** (adjacent or overlapping). The other half of the Tow Truck card dictates the new open color for opponents!
* **The Red Light (2 Cards - Stop & Honk):**
  * Shows a bright glowing red traffic light with a STOP sign.
  * When placed, the player slams the table and yells *"RED LIGHT! BRAKE CHECK!"*
  * The next player is **skipped** (they must honk their imaginary horn and pass).

---

## 7. Winning the Race

* **The 1-Card Warning:** When a player has only **1 card remaining in their hand**, they must loudly honk twice: *"BEEP BEEP!"* (If an opponent catches them before their next turn, they must draw 1 penalty card from the Junkyard).
* **The Checkered Flag:** The first player to legally play their final card from their hand wins the game!
* Everyone celebrates with a giant round of engine revving!

---

## 8. Tabletop Footprint & Space Control

Because 5-year-olds play on coffee tables, dining tables, or rugs:
1. **Overlapping compresses space:** Half of all plays pile on top of existing cards rather than extending outwards.
2. **90° turns wrap around:** When the road hits an edge, 90-degree corner seams force the traffic to turn back toward the center of the table.
3. **True Matches clear the table:** Every time a True Match drives off, cards leave the table, guaranteeing the traffic jam never spills onto the floor!
