# 🚨 Caught Red-Handed

**A Fast-Paced Family Heist Game of Banking Loot & Getting Away Clean**  
*Players*: 2–6 (Optimized for 4) | *Ages*: 7+ | *Play Time*: 8–10 mins | *Components*: 56 Cards

---

## 1. The Heist Premise
You and your crew just pulled off a daring robbery. You want to walk away with the biggest haul of stolen loot banked in front of you. 

Laundering **Red Contraband** scores huge points because hot evidence is worth more on the black market! But be careful: if any player empties their hand and shouts **"GETAWAY CAR!"**, anyone caught holding **even a single Red card** is **CAUGHT RED-HANDED**—and their **entire banked haul for the round is confiscated (0 points)!**

---

## 2. Deck Anatomy (56 Cards)

| Card Group | Count | Details & Values |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Clean Loot (Blue)** | **28** | 7 Items (Cash, Diamonds, Watches, Keys, Paintings, Gold, Crowns). **Value: 1 pt each**. |
| **Petty Contraband (Red)** | **12** | Cash, Diamonds, Watches, Keys. **Value: 3 pts each**. |
| **Grand Contraband (Red)** | **6** | Paintings, Gold Bars, Crowns. **Value: 5 pts each**. |
| **The Smoking Gun 🔫** | **2** | **The Bear!** Unpairable, cannot be laundered. Pure 100% bust evidence. **Value: 0 pts**. |
| **"Look Out!" (Reverse)** | **6** | Action card. Flips the Security Camera direction of play. |
| **Table Markers** | **2** | 1 Flippable **Security Camera** card (Clockwise / Counter-Clockwise) + 1 Quick Reference Card. |

---

## 3. Setup (30 Seconds)
1. Place the **Security Camera** card in the center pointing **Clockwise (Pass Left)**.
2. Deal **5 cards** face-down to each player.
3. The remaining cards form the face-down **Evidence Locker** (draw pile).
4. **Opening Drop**: All players check their starting hands and immediately lay any initial pairs face-up in front of them into their **Personal Score Stash**.

---

## 4. Turn Structure

On your turn, execute these **3 steps** in order:

```
[1. DRAW] ──▶ [2. LAY PAIRS] ──▶ [3. PASS IN DIRECTION OF PLAY]
```

### Step 1: Draw (The Evidence Locker)
Draw **1 card** from the center Evidence Locker.  
*(If the Locker runs out, shuffle the Vault discard pile to form a fresh Locker).*

### Step 2: Lay Pairs (Your Personal Stash)
Check your hand for any matching pairs of the same item and lay them face-up in front of you:
* **Clean Pair (Blue + Blue)**: Banks points equal to **1 + 1 = 2 Points**.
* **Laundered Pair (Blue + Red)**: Banks points equal to **1 + 3 = 4 Points** (or **1 + 5 = 6 Points** for Grand Loot)!
* **"Look Out!" (Reverse)**: Play this card to the center Vault. Physically flip the Security Camera to immediately reverse the direction of play!

### Step 3: Pass in Direction of Play
If you have at least 1 card in hand, choose **1 card** and slide it face-down to your neighbor in the active direction (Left if Clockwise, Right if Counter-Clockwise).  
* **Passing your last card is 100% legal!** If you pass your final card away, you just dumped evidence on your neighbor and emptied your hand!

---

## 5. End of Round & Scoring

The split second a player empties their hand (either by laying pairs or by passing their last card away):

```
🚗💨 "GETAWAY CAR!"
```

The round ends immediately! **FREEZE!**

1. **The Escape Check**: All other players reveal their remaining hands:
   * **Clean Hand (Zero Red Cards & No Smoking Gun)**:  
     You slip away clean! Count up all the points on your banked pairs in front of you and add them to your score!
   * **Caught Red-Handed (Holding 1+ Red Cards or The Smoking Gun)**:  
     🚨 **BUSTED!** The sirens wail, the cops swarm, and **your entire banked stash this round is seized (0 Points)!**
2. **The Getaway Driver**: The player who emptied their hand earns all their banked points **+ a 3-Point Getaway Bonus**!

### Winning the Game
Play **3 rounds** (or first player to **25 points**). The player with the highest total score wins the heist!
