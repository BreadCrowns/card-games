# Caught Red-Handed

**A Fast-Paced Family Card Game of Planting Evidence & Coming Clean**  
*Players*: 2–6 (Optimized for 4) | *Ages*: 7+ | *Play Time*: 8–10 minutes | *Components*: 56 Cards

---

## 1. The Premise
A high-stakes heist just went sideways. The room is hot, the police sirens are echoing, and everyone is clutching stolen goods. The goal is simple: **get rid of all your cards and shout "I'M CLEAN!"** before someone else does. 

Beware of **Red Contraband cards**—they are hot evidence! You cannot pair them with other Red cards, so you must either wash them with matching Clean loot or sneakily pass them to your neighbor. If someone goes clean while you are still holding Red cards, you are **CAUGHT RED-HANDED!**

---

## 2. Deck Anatomy (56 Cards)

| Card Group | Count | Details |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Clean Loot** | **38** | 7–8 Items (Cash, Diamonds, Watches, Paintings, Gold Bars, Crowns, Keys, Gems) with 4–5 clean copies each. |
| **Red Contraband** | **8** | Bright crimson contraband items. Can **only** be discarded when paired with a matching Clean item of the same type (Laundromat). |
| **"Look Out!" (Reverse)** | **8** | Single-card action. When played, immediately flip the **Security Camera** in the center—passing direction reverses! |
| **Centerpiece / Reference** | **2** | 1 double-sided **Security Camera** indicator (Clockwise / Counter-Clockwise) + 1 Quick Reference Card. |

---

## 3. Setup
1. Place the **Security Camera** card in the center of the table pointing **CLOCKWISE (Pass Left)**.
2. Shuffle the deck and deal **5 cards** face-down to each player (4 cards for a faster 5-minute game).
3. The remaining cards form the face-down **Evidence Locker** (draw pile) in the center.
4. **Initial Shed**: Before the first turn, all players check their starting hands and immediately discard any matching pairs face-up into the **Vault** (discard pile).

---

## 4. Turn Structure

On your turn, execute these 3 steps in order:

### Step 1: Draw (The Evidence Locker)
Draw **1 card** from the center Evidence Locker into your hand.  
*(If the Locker ever runs out, shuffle the Vault discard pile to form a fresh Locker).*

### Step 2: Pair & Discard (The Vault)
Check your hand for any valid plays:
* **Clean Pair**: Discard 2 matching Clean items (e.g., Blue Watch + Blue Watch).
* **Laundered Pair**: Discard 1 Red item + 1 matching Clean item (e.g., Red Diamond + Blue Diamond). You just washed away the evidence!
* **"Look Out!" (Reverse)**: Discard this card by slapping it onto the table. Physically flip the center Security Camera to reverse the passing direction!

> **The Golden Win Rule**: You can **only** win by discarding a pair that empties your hand to **0 cards**. The moment you do, slam the table and shout **"I'M CLEAN!"** (The round ends immediately!).

### Step 3: The Plant (Pass to Neighbor)
If you still have 2 or more cards in hand, choose **1 card** from your hand and slide it face-down to your neighbor in the active direction (Left if clockwise, Right if counter-clockwise).  
*They must take it into their hand.*  
*(Note: You can never pass yourself down to 0 cards without pairing).*

---

## 5. End of Round & Scoring

The round ends the split second a player shouts **"I'M CLEAN!"**
* **FREEZE!** All other players immediately reveal their remaining hands.
* **Count Red Evidence**:
  * Safe (Blue) cards left in hand count as **0 points** (they are clean loot!).
  * Every **Red Contraband card** held in hand scores **1 Penalty Point (Red Mark)**.
* The player who shouted "I'M CLEAN!" scores **0 points**.

### Winning the Game
Play **3 rounds**. The player with the fewest Red Marks wins the heist!
