# 🚨 Caught Red-Handed: Classic Hustle Edition

**A Fast-Paced Family Heist Game of Banking Loot & Getting Away Clean**  
*Players*: 2–6 (Optimized for 4) | *Ages*: 7+ | *Match Length*: ~20–30 mins (First to 200 pts) | *Components*: 56 Cards

---

## 1. The Heist Premise
You and your crew just pulled off a daring robbery. You want to walk away with the biggest haul of loot banked in front of you across multiple heist rounds.

* Laundering **Red Contraband** scores massive points—up to **20 points in a single pair**!
* But beware: if any player empties their hand and shouts **"GETAWAY CAR!"**, anyone caught holding **even a single Red card** is **CAUGHT RED-HANDED**—and their **entire banked haul for that round is seized (0 points)!**
* Watch out for **Green Fakes** (Counterfeits worth 0 pts) circulating through the crew—they gum up your hand, can't be paired together, and get passed like hot potatoes!

---

## 2. Deck Anatomy (56 Cards)

| Card Group | Count | Details & Values |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Clean Loot (Blue)** | **24** | 7 Items (Cash, Diamonds, Watches, Keys, Paintings, Gold, Crowns). **Value: 5 pts each**. |
| **Petty Contraband (Red)** | **10** | Cash, Diamonds, Watches, Keys. **Value: 10 pts each**. |
| **Grand Contraband (Red)** | **6** | Paintings, Gold Bars, Crowns. **Value: 15 pts each**! |
| **Fakes / Counterfeit (Green)** | **8** | Prop Cash, Fake Diamonds, Knockoff Watches, Forged Art, Fool's Gold. **Value: 0 pts each**. |
| **"Look Out!" (Reverse)** | **6** | Action card. Flips the Security Camera direction of play. |
| **Table Markers** | **2** | 1 Flippable **Security Camera** card (Clockwise / Counter-Clockwise) + 1 Match Score / Ref Card. |

---

## 3. Setup (30 Seconds)
1. Place the **Security Camera** card in the center pointing **Clockwise (Pass Left)**.
2. Deal **7 cards** face-down to each player.
3. The remaining cards form the face-down **Evidence Locker** (draw pile).
4. **No Free Starting Pairs**: There is no pre-game discard. All players keep their full 7-card hand and must wait for their turn to bank pairs!

---

## 4. Turn Structure

On your turn, execute these **3 steps** in order:

```
[1. DRAW] ──▶ [2. LAY AT MOST 1 PAIR] ──▶ [3. PASS IN DIRECTION OF PLAY]
```

### Step 1: Draw (The Evidence Locker)
Draw **1 card** from the center Evidence Locker.  
*(If the Locker runs out, shuffle any played Reverse cards in the Vault. Stashed pairs on the table stay banked).*

### Step 2: Lay At Most 1 Pair (Your Personal Stash)
Check your hand for matching items. **You may lay at most 1 pair per turn**:
* **Clean Pair (Blue + Blue)**: Banks **5 + 5 = 10 Points**!
* **Laundered Petty Pair (Blue + Red)**: Banks **5 + 10 = 15 Points**!
* **Laundered Grand Pair (Blue + Red)**: Banks **5 + 15 = 20 Points**!
* **Counterfeit Pair (Blue + Green Fake)**: Banks **5 + 0 = 5 Points**!
  * *Rule on Fakes*: Green cards **cannot** pair with another Green card. You must burn a matching Clean (Blue) card to lay down a counterfeit pair. While it only banks 5 pts, it sheds 2 cards from your hand!
* **"Look Out!" (Reverse)**: If you play a Reverse, discard it to the Vault and flip the Security Camera to immediately reverse play direction!

> *Note: Illegal pairs include Red + Red (two contrabands cannot launder each other) and Green + Green (fakes need real items).*

### Step 3: Pass in Direction of Play
Choose **1 card** from your hand and slide it face-down to your neighbor in the active direction (Left if Clockwise, Right if Counter-Clockwise).  
* **Passing your last card is 100% legal!** If passing leaves your hand empty, you just planted evidence on your neighbor and emptied your hand to trigger the getaway!

---

## 5. End of Round & Scoring

The split second a player empties their hand:

```
🚗💨 "GETAWAY CAR!"
```

The round ends immediately! **FREEZE!**

1. **The Escape Check**: All other players reveal their remaining hands:
   * **Clean Hand (Zero Red Cards)**:  
     You slip away clean! Count up all the points on your banked pairs in front of you and add them to your match score! *(Green cards in hand are worth 0 pts, but they do NOT cause a bust).*
   * **Caught Red-Handed (Holding 1+ Red Cards)**:  
     🚨 **BUSTED!** The sirens wail, the cops swarm, and **your entire banked stash this round is seized (0 Points)!**
2. **The Getaway Driver**: The player who emptied their hand earns all their banked points **+ a 15-Point Getaway Bonus**!

---

## 6. Winning the Game (First to 200 Points)

Add each player's round score to their cumulative match total on the scorecard:
* If at least one player reaches **200 points**, the heist series ends immediately! The player with the highest total score is crowned the **Master Thief**!
* If no player has reached 200 points, gather all 54 cards, rotate the dealer/first player, deal 7 fresh cards, and start the next round! (Typically takes **~5 rounds** of fast-paced play).
