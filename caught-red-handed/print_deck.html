HEISTTurn Guide
RULES
1. DRAW • 2. MELD • 3. PASS
• 1. Draw 1 card from the Locker.
• 2. Meld exactly 1 pair (optional).
  GOLDEN LAW: Every pair needs Blue!
  🔵+🔵 = 10 pts (Clean)
  🔵+🔴 Petty = 15 pts | Grand = 20 pts
  🔵+🎭 Wild = 5 pts & Flips Camera 🔄
• 3. Pass 1 card face-down to neighbor.
RED+RED & RED+GREEN ARE STRICTLY ILLEGAL

FALL GUY
THE ESCAPE CHECK
• Getaway Driver (First empty hand) triggers the alarm! Keeps full stash.
• Caught Red-Handed: Player(s) holding the MOST Red Contraband take the fall = 0 POINTS!
• Evaded Arrest: Anyone holding fewer Red cards than the leader slips away and banks full stash!
• Target: First to 300 pts (200 in 2P Duel).
Avoid holding the most Red contraband!
