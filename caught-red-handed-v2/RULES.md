# 🚨 Caught Red-Handed V2: The Heat

**A Cutthroat Shedding & Climbing Game of Dumping Contraband & Surviving the Feds**  
*Players*: 2–6 (Optimized for 3–5) | *Ages*: 8+ | *Match Length*: ~15–25 mins | *Components*: 54 Play Cards + 2 Reference Cards

---

## 1. The Core Premise: "If You're Caught Red-Handed, You're Out!"

You and your crew are fleeing a high-profile heist with police sirens closing in. To escape, you must **shed every card from your hand**.

* There is **one central Discard Pile** that constantly climbs higher.
* On your turn, you must discard a card with a **strictly higher number** than the top card of the pile.
* **The Catch**: All **Red Contraband cards are LOW numbers (1, 2, 3)**! Once the pile climbs into the high Blue numbers (4 through 10), your hot contraband is **trapped in your hand**!
* **The Reset Cards**: Special cards (The Fence, Smoke Bomb, The Snitch) reset the pile back to **0**, opening a brief, desperate window to dump your low Red cards!
* **If you can't play, you must draw a card**.
* The moment any player sheds their last card, the Getaway Car speeds off! Everyone else reveals their hand:
  * 🚨 **If you are caught holding ANY Red Contraband, YOU ARE CAUGHT RED-HANDED!** (You take a Mugshot Strike).
  * 3 Strikes and you are **ELIMINATED**! Last thief standing wins!

---

## 2. Deck Anatomy (54 Play Cards)

The deck consists of 54 cards divided into three clear groups:

| Card Group | Value / Rank | Count | Description & Strategic Role |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Contraband (Red)** | **1, 2, 3** | **12** | **Toxic Evidence!** 4 copies of each rank (1: Petty Pickpocket, 2: Hot Jewelry, 3: Blood Diamond). Low numbers make them difficult to shed once the pile climbs! |
| **Clean Loot (Blue)** | **4 through 10** | **34** | **Safe Ladders.** 5 copies each of 4–9 (Cash, Keys, Art, Gold, Crowns, Relics) + 4 copies of 10 (Priceless Diamond). Easy to climb the pile and choke out opponents! |
| **Reset Cards (Special)** | **0 / Wild** | **8** | **Tactical Resets.** Resets the pile count back to 0 so low Red cards can be played: |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;• **The Fence** | **0** | **4** | Dumps hot loot! Resets pile count to 0. Next player may play any card ($\ge 1$). |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;• **Smoke Bomb** | **0 🔄** | **2** | Resets pile count to 0 **AND reverses direction of play** (↻ ↔ ↺)! |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;• **The Snitch** | **0 🚨** | **2** | Resets pile count to 0 **AND forces the next player to draw 1 card**! |

---

## 3. Setup (30 Seconds)

1. Shuffle the 54-card deck thoroughly.
2. Deal **7 cards** face-down to each player.
3. Place the remaining cards face-down in the center as the **Evidence Locker** (Draw Pile).
4. Flip the top card of the Locker face-up to start the **Center Discard Pile**:
   * *If the starting card is a Reset card (0), it starts at 0.*
   * *If it's a number card, play starts against that number.*
5. Set play direction **Clockwise** (Pass/Play Left). Give each player **3 Strike tokens** (or track on a Mugshot sheet).

---

## 4. Turn Structure

On your turn, execute **one** of two choices:

### Choice A: Play 1 Valid Card
You may play **1 card** from your hand onto the Center Discard Pile if it meets either condition:

1. **Higher Number**: The card has a **strictly higher numerical value** than the top card of the Discard Pile.
   * *Example*: Top card is a **5**. You may play a **6, 7, 8, 9, or 10**.
   * *Note*: You **cannot** play a 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 on a 5!
2. **Special Reset Card (Value 0)**: You may play **The Fence**, **Smoke Bomb**, or **The Snitch** on **ANY** card!
   * The active pile count immediately resets to **0**.
   * Resolve any special effect (Reversing play direction or forcing the next player to draw).
   * The next player is now free to play any card ($1$ through $10$), finally opening the window for low Red Contraband!

---

### Choice B: Draw 1 Card (Pass)
If you cannot play a higher card (or choose to hold your cards):
1. **Draw 1 card** from the Evidence Locker (Draw Pile) into your hand.
2. If the drawn card is legally playable, you **may immediately play it**!
3. If you still cannot play (or choose not to), your turn ends and play passes to the next player.

*(If the Locker draw pile ever runs dry, reshuffle all cards in the discard pile except the top active card to form a fresh draw pile).*

---

## 5. End of Round: The Escape Check

A round ends immediately the instant **any player plays their very last card**! That player shouts:

> 🚗💨 **"GETAWAY CAR!"**

Play stops instantly. All remaining players must **FREEZE** and reveal their entire remaining hand face-up on the table:

1. **The Getaway Driver**:
   * The player who emptied their hand escapes scot-free! (0 Strikes).
2. **Caught Red-Handed (Holding ANY Red Contraband)**:
   * 🚨 **BUSTED!** Any player holding **at least one Red Contraband card (1, 2, or 3)** was caught red-handed with the evidence!
   * They receive **1 Mugshot Strike**!
   * *(Cruel Variant / Sudden Death: If playing sudden-death, anyone caught with Red cards is immediately eliminated!)*
3. **Escaped Clean (Holding ONLY Blue / Reset cards)**:
   * 🛡️ Even if you didn't empty your hand, if you successfully dumped all your Red Contraband and only hold Blue cards or Resets, the feds have no evidence on you! **You escape clean with 0 Strikes!**

---

## 6. Winning the Game

* Players start with **3 Clean Lives** (3 Mugshot Strikes allowed).
* When a player receives their **3rd Strike**, they are **SENT TO PRISON (Eliminated)**!
* The **last remaining player standing** who has not been eliminated wins the game and is crowned the **Master Thief**!

---

## 7. Strategic Depth & Table Tactics

* **The Blue Ladder Climb**: When you see your neighbor holding many cards, play high Blue numbers ($7, 8, 9, 10$) to choke out their low Red Contraband and force them to draw!
* **The Reset Ambush**: Save your Reset cards (The Fence, Smoke Bomb) for when you hold multiple Red cards. Dropping a Reset resets the pile to 0, allowing you to drop a Red 1 on your turn and forcing opponents to climb from the bottom!
* **Smoke Bomb Defense**: If the player before you dropped a high 10, drop a Smoke Bomb (0 🔄) to reverse play direction right back to them, forcing them to face a 0 lead!
