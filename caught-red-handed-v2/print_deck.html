Turn Guide
V2 CORE
CLIMBING & SHEDDING
• On your turn, execute 1 choice:
  1. Play 1 Card > current top discard
  -OR- Play Reset Card (0) on any card!
  2. Draw 1 Card from Locker if unable/unwilling to play.
• If drawn card is legal, you may play it immediately!
