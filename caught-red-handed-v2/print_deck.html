🚨 TURN REFERENCE
CAUGHT RED-HANDED V2
ON YOUR TURN, CHOOSE 1:
1. PLAY & DECLARE: Play face-down & declare number > previous (or declare Reset).
2. INVESTIGATE!: Challenge the previous player's card.
3. PASS & DRAW: Draw 1 card from locker. Turn ends.
🔍 INVESTIGATION VERDICT:
• ILLEGAL / LIE: Bluffer takes ALL Red cards from evidence pile into Penalty Pile!
• LEGAL / TRUTH: Investigator takes ALL Red cards into Penalty Pile!
(Blue & Reset cards are secured to Vault).
🚗 GETAWAY CAR (ROUND END):
First to empty hand escapes (0 Strikes)! Remaining players count:
(Red in Hand) + (Red in Penalty Pile).
Most Red cards = 1 Strike! 3 Strikes = PRISON!
📋 DECK ARCHITECTURE
54 CARDS TOTAL
• 36 CLEAN LOOT (BLUE 1-10): Safe goods. Zero penalty points.

• 8 NUMBERED RED (1-6): Low contraband. Can be played legally if > previous number!

• 6 HOT EVIDENCE (🚨 RED): Unnumbered toxic contraband. CANNOT match any number! Must bluff to shed!

• 4 RESETS (0 SPECIAL): Resets count to 0. The Fence (0 🤝) & Smoke Bomb (0 💨🔄 reverses play).
💡 TIP: Call bluff when the pile has few red cards; avoid false accusations when the pile is loaded!
