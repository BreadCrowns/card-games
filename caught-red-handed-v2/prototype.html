How To Play: The Interrogation

Goal: Empty your hand first to trigger the Getaway Car, while sticking your rivals with toxic Red Contraband!

On Your Turn, Choose ONE:
  • Play Face Down & Declare: Choose 1 card, place it face-down, and declare its value. The declared value must be strictly higher than the previous declared value (> X). Or declare "Reset" with a Reset card!
  • INVESTIGATE!: Call bluff on the previous player's card!
  • Pass & Draw: Draw 1 card from locker. Turn passes.
🔍 Investigation Resolution:
  • Caught Red-Handed (Bluff / Illegal): Bluffer takes ALL Red Contraband cards from the pile into their Penalty Pile!
  • False Accusation (Truth / Legal): Investigator takes ALL Red Contraband cards from the pile!
  • Blue & Reset cards are secured into the Vault (cleared)!
🚨 Red Contraband Types:
  • Numbered Red (1-6): Low contraband. Can be played legally if > previous number!
  • Hot Evidence (🚨): NO NUMBER! You CANNOT play it legally. You MUST BLUFF to shed it!
🚗 Getaway Car & Strikes:

When any player empties their hand, the round ends. The escapee gets 0 Strikes! Everyone else counts: Hand Red + Penalty Red. Highest red count takes 1 Strike! 3 Strikes = Federal Prison!
